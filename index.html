RFID test tools

Tools for testing RFID devices in the browser (Chrome or Edge, over USB), with command-line versions. Source: mfcarroll/rfid-test-tools.

Chameleon Ultra

Each tool needs its own test firmware, available to flash on the page.

Crystal oscillator offset How fast or slow the 32 MHz crystal runs, measured against internet time. FSK self-trim (experimental) Emulates HID Prox, Paradox and ioProx cards matched to a reader's clock. Put the same card in slots with and without the various experimental modes, and see which ones a reader accepts.

Flipper Zero

Crystal oscillator calibration Measures the 32 MHz crystal against internet time and saves the correction in a new version of the RFID detector app, allowing exact LF reader frequency measurements.
